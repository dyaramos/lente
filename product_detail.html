Getting Started

  1. Charge the battery fully before first use.
  2. Insert an SD card (UHS-II recommended).
  3. Attach the RF-S lens and power on the camera.
  4. Set date, time, and image quality in the menu.

Glossary

Dual Pixel CMOS AF
Canon's autofocus technology that uses every pixel on the sensor for phase-detection focusing.
APS-C
A sensor format smaller than full-frame, giving a 1.6x crop factor.
